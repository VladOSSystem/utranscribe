01 / READ

Every available word.

Read the transcript alongside your video. Search for a phrase, jump to a timestamp, or keep a TXT copy.

02 / CHOOSE

Your favorite AI.

Select ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini in Settings. No API keys or extension account to configure.

03 / SUMMARIZE

Ready when you are.

Open your AI with the transcript and summary prompt filled in. Review the message and press Send yourself.

A few things to know

Captions must be available on YouTube. utranscribe does not record or transcribe video audio. Your AI provider may require sign-in and applies its own usage and message-length limits.
